Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026. This policy applies to WakeMao on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Your tasks and sync

Tasks, notes, anniversaries, habit check-ins, bill records and settings are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, supported data syncs through your private CloudKit database. The developer does not operate a separate account system or task-storage server.

Voice and parsing

Voice input requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. Recognition runs on device when supported; otherwise Apple speech services may process audio. If you enable DeepSeek with your own API key, the input being parsed and current-date context are sent to DeepSeek, not your entire task database. Your key is stored in the system Keychain. DeepSeek processing and fees are subject to its own terms.

Location and weather

When you enable weather and grant location permission, coordinates are rounded to two decimal places before being sent to Apple through WeatherKit for forecasts. Apple Maps services resolve the city name and time zone. The app caches approximate coordinates, the city and weather locally, without retaining a location history or sending task content to the weather service. When location permission is revoked, the app clears its weather cache. Apple states that WeatherKit location information is used only for forecasts, is not associated with personally identifiable information and is not tracked between requests. See WeatherKit privacy information and weather data sources.

Exchange rates and external services

Bill conversion downloads public USD exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) and caches them locally. Requests do not include bill names, amounts or records. Online services receive connection information such as your IP address as part of normal network requests; their own privacy policies apply.

Voluntary support and payments

One-time and yearly support do not unlock features. Apple processes payments through the App Store; WakeMao does not receive payment-card details. The app reads Apple-verified product, transaction and subscription status to complete purchases and show yearly support status. One-time support cannot be restored; restoration applies only to active yearly support.

Automatic yearly renewal

Yearly support is an auto-renewing one-year subscription at the local price shown by the App Store when purchasing. It renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges for renewal within the final 24 hours. Manage or cancel in your Apple account subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Promotion and tracking

The app has no advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer’s app catalog is bundled. Opening a product connects to Apple’s store, and missing icons may be downloaded from Apple image services. External websites and the App Store have their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

You can delete content in the app. Some deletions are retained as deletion markers to support sync. Deleting the app removes local data but does not automatically clear iCloud data or Apple purchase records. Manage iCloud data through Apple’s iCloud storage controls. Exported or separately copied data must be deleted separately.

Support and contact

For help or privacy requests, contact wallyvay@gmail.com. Include your device, OS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send API keys, passwords, payment-card details or private task contents. No login is required. Notification permission is used for due-date reminders.
